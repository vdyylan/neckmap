Ratios use the WCAG 2 formula. Note markers need 3:1 against what surrounds them (SC 1.4.11) and label text needs 4.5:1 against its marker (SC 1.4.3). When a colour falls below 3:1 against the board, the marker gets a dark outline, and the outline is what carries the contrast. Layers also differ by shape (circle, square, diamond), so colour is never the only cue. Colours come from the Okabe–Ito colour-blind-safe palette.
